Verified Memory pack maker

Turn your ChatGPT or Claude data export into a memory pack: pick the exact sentences worth remembering, and download a pack whose every quote is checked word for word against your chats.

Your export never leaves this browser. There is no server, no analytics, and this page is not allowed to make network requests. You can also save the page and use it offline.

How do I get my export?
  • ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → Export data. You get an email with a .zip.
  • Claude: Settings → Privacy → Export data. You get an email with a .zip.
  • Also accepted: a messages.jsonl in the format of verified-memory-build-pack.
